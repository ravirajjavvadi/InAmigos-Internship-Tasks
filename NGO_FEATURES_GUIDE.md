# NGO WEBSITE: ELITE FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS

This document outlines the 5 critical features identified for the InAmigos Foundation platform to drive transparency, engagement, and social impact.

---

### 🟢 1. DYNAMIC IMPACT DASHBOARD
- **Description**: A hero-section counter that displays real-time statistics of foundation achievements.
- **Why it matters**: Builds immediate credibility. Donors want to see that their contributions are part of a larger, successful mission.
- **Implementation**: Simple animated numbers (e.g., "1,200+ Students Educated", "$50k Total Aid").

### 🟢 2. STRATEGIC TIERED DONATION PORTAL
- **Description**: Modern donation cards that translate currency into specific impact outcomes.
- **Why it matters**: It removes the "how much should I give?" friction. By showing "$20 = 1 Week of Meals", you provide a tangible connection to the cause.
- **Implementation**: Glassmorphic cards with quick-select amounts and monthly subscription toggles.

### 🟢 3. VISUAL CAMPAIGN PROGRESS TRACKER
- **Description**: Real-time progress bars for specific, time-bound missions or infrastructure projects.
- **Why it matters**: Creates a sense of urgency and collective accomplishment. Users are more likely to donate when they see a goal is "85% Complete."
- **Implementation**: Progress bars integrated with a "Goal vs. Current" data feed.

### 🟢 4. SMART VOLUNTEER MATCHMAKING
- **Description**: A skill-based onboarding form that asks volunteers for their expertise (e.g., "Teaching", "Coding", "Marketing").
- **Why it matters**: Ensures the foundation gets the right help for the right task, making the volunteer experience more rewarding.
- **Implementation**: Interactive multi-step form with logic-based field mapping.

### 🟢 5. VERIFIED STORYTELLING WALL
- **Description**: A grid of high-fidelity media cards featuring stories and testimonials from beneficiaries.
- **Why it matters**: Storytelling is the most powerful tool for NGOs. It humanizes the data and build deep emotional bonds with supporters.
- **Implementation**: Image/Video cards with "Read Full Story" links leading to deep-dive articles.
