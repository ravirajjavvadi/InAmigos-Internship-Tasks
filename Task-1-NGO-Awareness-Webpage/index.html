About InAmigos Foundation

Driven by Purpose.
Led by Impact.

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization committed to creating lasting social impact.

With its base in Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates across India, addressing critical societal issues through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers.
