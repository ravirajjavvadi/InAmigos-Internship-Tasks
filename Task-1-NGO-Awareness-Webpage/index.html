About InAmigos Foundation

Driven by Purpose.
Led by Impact.

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization authorized by the Central Government of India.

Our foundation operates with a clear vision: to catalyze sustainable social change through technology-driven solutions, community engagement, and transparent operations. With its base in Chhattisgarh, we coordinate a massive network of dedicated professionals and over 30,000 interns nationwide.

Mr. Govind Shukla Founder & CEO

Our Core Mission

To empower underprivileged communities by providing access to quality education, healthcare, and sustainable livelihood opportunities through innovative social engineering.
